Three-class taxonomy

        
Not all uncertainty is the same.

      

      

        

          KNOWN
          
Confident & consistent

          
A well-specified question with a definitive answer. Samples converge into one dominant semantic cluster with low entropy.

          
“What is 2 + 2?”

        

        

          AMBIGUOUS
          
Many valid readings

          
The question has several materially different interpretations. Sampling reveals competing clusters, each internally coherent.

          
“What is the capital of Georgia?”

        

        

          UNKNOWN
          
Beyond what it can know

          
The question is clear, but the answer is inaccessible — private thoughts, future events. Responses disperse or refuse.

          
“What number am I thinking of?”

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
How it works

        
Ask once. Sample many. Measure the spread.

      

      
    
        
  1. 01
    Adaptive routing
    A greedy first answer is routed: simple, confident questions take a fast path; everything else is escalated.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    Stochastic sampling
    The local LLM produces a set of responses at temperature 0.7 through Ollama.
    3. 
        
  3. 03
    Semantic clustering
    Responses are embedded and grouped with agglomerative clustering on cosine similarity.
    4. 
        
  4. 04
    16 features
    Entropy, agreement, margins, similarity, lexical diversity, hedging and negation are extracted.
    5. 
        
  5. 05
    Classification
    A trained classifier predicts KNOWN / AMBIGUOUS / UNKNOWN, guarded by ambiguity and inaccessibility checks.
